# Subcontract Award Policy

Teton County Justice Center - FGMP / 90% Construction Documents

> SYNTHETIC TEST DOCUMENT. Written for bid-leveling POC testing. It is not the actual policy of any contractor. The rules are chosen to be consistent with the awards recorded in the TCJC leveling workbook.

## 1. Purpose

**1.1** This policy sets out how subcontract bid packages for the Teton County Justice Center are leveled and awarded. It applies to every trade package bid at the 90% Construction Documents stage.

## 2. Basis of award

**2.1** Award is recommended to the lowest responsive and responsible bidder, measured on Leveled Total Package Value, not on the price as submitted.

**2.2** Leveled Total Package Value = bid as submitted + CM/GC plugs for scope gaps = Base Bid Value; + post-bid adjustments (Textura) = Subcontract Value; + sales tax and SDI = Total Package Value.

**2.3** A bidder within 3% of the lowest leveled total may be recommended instead on best-value grounds (schedule, warranty, safety, staffing) with written justification from the package lead.

## 3. Responsive bids

**3.1** A bid is responsive if it was received by the bid deadline, is priced against the current documents including all addenda, and the bidder answers CM/GC clarification questions within two (2) business days.

**3.2** Clarification questions are sent to the bidders the package lead considers in contention for award. They are recorded in the bid clarification log. A bidder who was not sent questions remains responsive.

**3.3** A bidder that does not answer clarification questions by the deadline is marked UNRESPONSIVE and is excluded from award, whatever its price.

**3.4** When a bidder submits more than one bid, only the latest dated bid is leveled. Earlier bids are kept on file as superseded.

**3.5** Bids are requested to remain valid for 60 days. A shorter validity is flagged but does not make a bid non-responsive.

## 4. Responsible bidders

**4.1** The recommended bidder must hold prequalification status "Approved" or "Approved with conditions" at award. Conditions must be closed in writing before the subcontract is issued.

**4.2** The Total Package Value may not exceed the bidder's single-project limit on the prequalification roster.

**4.3** Predicted Safety Incidents score: 5.0 or below is acceptable; above 5.0 and up to 15.0 requires a safety mitigation plan; above 15.0 requires written approval from the regional safety manager before award.

## 5. Leveling rules

**5.1** Scope gaps, allowances, quantity differences and unit conversions are leveled according to the Plugging Policy (plugging_policy.yaml and plugging_policy.md), which governs over this section.

**5.2** Textura: 0.221% of Base Bid Value. SDI: 1.45% of Subcontract Value.

**5.3** Sales tax: the project is tax exempt; bids are leveled exclusive of sales tax.

## 6. Low-bid review

**6.1** A scope review is mandatory for any bid whose Leveled Total Package Value is more than 20% below the CM/GC estimate, or more than 15% below the next lowest bidder.

## 7. Warranty minimums

**7.1** Bidders must meet the warranties in the contract documents. Minimums for the packages in this sample set:

**7.2** 07.50 Roofing - TPO roofing system (RA1, RA2): 20-year manufacturer no-dollar-limit (NDL) total-system warranty.

**7.3** 07.50 Roofing - Roofing installation: 2-year installer workmanship warranty.

**7.4** 07.50 Roofing - Sheet metal flashing and trim: 20-year finish warranty.

**7.5** 09.67 Fluid-Applied Flooring - Resinous flooring (RN-1, RN-2) and integral cove base: 5-year joint manufacturer/installer warranty, material and labor.

**7.6** 09.67 Fluid-Applied Flooring - Traffic coating (TC-1): 5-year joint manufacturer/installer warranty.

**7.7** 09.67 Fluid-Applied Flooring - Polished and sealed concrete (PSC-1, SC-1, SC-2, SC-4): 1-year workmanship warranty.

**7.8** 32.90 Landscaping and Irrigation - Trees, shrubs, perennials and sod: 1-year replacement warranty from Substantial Completion, no exclusions for winter kill.

**7.9** 32.90 Landscaping and Irrigation - Irrigation system: 1-year warranty including first winterization and spring start-up.

**7.10** 32.90 Landscaping and Irrigation - Unit pavers: 2-year warranty against settlement and displacement.

**7.11** 32.90 Landscaping and Irrigation - Site furnishings: Manufacturer's standard warranty, 3 years minimum.

**7.12** A warranty shortfall must be corrected in writing before award. If the bidder will not correct it, the cost of an equivalent warranty is added as a plug.

## 8. Alternates

**8.1** Alternates are not part of the leveled comparison. They are reported separately for each bidder.

## 9. Combination and all-or-nothing bids

**9.1** A bidder who will not allow its price to be split across packages is flagged. The bid may still be recommended where the whole of its price falls in one package.

## 10. Approval

**10.1** Recommendations up to $250,000 are approved by the project manager; from $250,000 to $1,000,000 by the project executive; above $1,000,000 by the regional preconstruction lead with Owner concurrence.
